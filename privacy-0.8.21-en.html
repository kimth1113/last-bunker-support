Last Bunker
Privacy policy

Google Play Android edition · Effective with 0.8.21 · Prepared November 6, 2026

Tason Mom handles personal information for Last Bunker: Shooting Defense (com.tason.lastbunker) as described below. This policy applies to the Google Play Android edition.

Information collected by the game

The game runs without registration. Game progress and battle records are not sent to a developer server. Android version 0.8.21 includes Google Mobile Ads for interstitials and optional rewarded retry ads and the UMP SDK for consent management. When ads are requested, Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers such as advertising IDs and app set IDs to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Requests may also occur while preparing an ad before viewing. Transmissions use TLS encryption. No separate developer analytics or payment SDK is used. Camera, microphone, contacts and location permissions are not requested. Vibration is used only for game feedback.

Records stored on your device

Game progress, best records, audio and display settings, paint tokens, owned paints and ad reward processing records are stored on your device. Paint tokens have no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. Token records are managed separately from progress backups and are not transferred by restoring a game backup. The developer cannot view or remotely restore these records. They are kept while you use the app and can be deleted by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Deleted progress cannot be recovered.

Ad age setting

From 0.8.2, first-time ad use asks you to choose under 16, 16–17, or 18 and older. No date of birth is requested. The age band is stored only on your device, not sent to a developer server. Change it in Settings → Data & support → Ad age setting. No ads are requested from the ad SDK before a choice is made. Google ad requests use child and teen protection settings for the selected age band. Under-16 users receive UMP's under-age-of-consent setting and Google's child-directed treatment; users aged 16–17 receive teen protections to restrict personalized ads. Protection settings are passed to Google's SDK. This does not verify your exact age or identity.

Support inquiries

For email inquiries, only your reply address and the content you send are used to investigate and respond. They are deleted within 90 days after the inquiry is resolved, except items that must legally be retained, which are kept for the required period. Do not send unnecessary identity documents, payment details or sensitive information. You can also send requests to access, correct or delete personal information to the address below.

Third parties and platforms

The developer does not sell game progress records. When delivering ads, the information described above may be sent to Google's ad services under Google's privacy policy. Google Play's own processing, including installations, updates and device diagnostics, follows Google's policies and your settings. This information page and the ad kill-switch configuration file are hosted on GitHub Pages. The app may fetch that file before requesting ads; connection information is subject to GitHub's privacy policy. These requests do not include game records or a separate user identifier.

Ads and privacy choices

From 0.8.21, paint-token ads are removed. The game uses interstitial ads between battles and optional rewarded retry ads. After a defeat, you can restart from Day 1 for free, or finish a rewarded ad for one retry using the state saved before that battle. Based on completed days, automatic interstitials do not appear on Days 1–5, appear after every two completions on Days 6–11, and after every completion from Day 12 during the shop transition. Practice, challenges and reopening the shop do not trigger automatic ads. If an ad is unavailable, the shop remains usable. Where required, a consent screen appears before ad requests. When available, change your choices in Settings → Data & support → Ad privacy choices. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If future ads, payments or server features change how information is handled, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect. Additional notice and consent will be provided in the app when required.
